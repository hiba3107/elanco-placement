Showing 14 cities and 1,000 sightings. How severity is worked out

  1. 1 Markers show four things: colour is severity, a ring means recent activity, a letter is the most-seen species, and size is the number of sightings. Each is a keyboard button.
  2. 2 The legend explains every visual code, so nothing relies on colour alone.
  3. 3 Filters for date range, species and severity. Quick ranges cover the common choices. The address bar keeps the filters, so a link reopens the same view.
  4. 4 The same cities as a list: an equivalent to the map for keyboard and screen-reader users, and a larger target than overlapping markers.
  5. 5 A banner explains when data is loading, saved from earlier, or unavailable.
  6. 6 A short welcome beside a drawing, with the key numbers in one quiet sentence rather than big number boxes.

  1. E Errors (red) appear after a first try at sending, as one summary that takes focus, with each problem repeated by its field.
  2. W Warnings (amber) appear as soon as they apply and never block sending.

6. Loading, empty and error states

Every box that loads data has all three, so no part of the page is ever blank without a reason.

1Loading sightings…

On the map and charts, the previous result stays visible, dimmed, while it updates.

2No sightings match these filters

Try a wider date range, or fewer species or severity levels.

Reset filters

3Couldn't load the sightings

Can't reach the Tick Tracker server. Check your connection.

Try again
  1. 1 Loading is announced to screen readers. The map and charts keep showing the last result, dimmed, until the new one arrives.
  2. 2 Empty says why and offers the way out.
  3. 3 Error says what failed in plain words and offers to try again. A chart whose library failed to load falls back to its table.
