## Adding a photo

1. **Check you may use it.** Use a photo you took yourself, or one whose licence allows reuse on a
   website, such as CC BY, CC BY-SA or CC0 (Wikimedia Commons has many), or a UK government image
   under the Open Government Licence. Do not use Elanco's logo or images without permission.
2. **Save it** in `web/images/photos/` as a JPEG or WebP, no more than about 1,600 pixels wide.
3. **Add it to the page with alt text** that says what it shows, for example
   `alt="A marsh tick on a fingertip, seen from above"`. A purely decorative photo gets `alt=""`.
4. **Credit it next to the photo**, in a caption, in the form its licence asks for, for example
   "Photo: Jane Smith, CC BY 4.0", with a link to the licence.
5. **Add a row below.**
